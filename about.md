# About

This blog collects short, readable technical reports from my "vibe research": quick experiments done together with AI assistants, written up so that someone outside the field can follow along.

Every post aims to have:

- **Plain-language explanations first**, with the math afterwards and every equation explained in words.
- **Real experiment results**: figures and tables come straight from the code and data linked in the post.
- **Checked references**: every cited paper is verified against arXiv, Crossref or Semantic Scholar before publishing, and the build refuses to publish unverified citations.
